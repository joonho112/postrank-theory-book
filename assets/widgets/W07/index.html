Domain 1 nevertheless loses a selected grade between alpha=3/40 and alpha=1/8. Maximum total disclosure is a separate objective, not the grade count of an arbitrary risk minimizer.

Two units; M = 1 in each domain; weak error includes target equality. Identity-only guards, no declared equality between distinct units, no mean caps.
