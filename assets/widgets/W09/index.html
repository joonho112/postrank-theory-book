More draws refine a probability; they do not change it

What shrinks when a fixed pair-error probability is computed from more independent draws?

Choose a setting to inspect its values. The table below records the initial example.

One fixed report states A above B under masses 4/5 at (1,0) and 1/5 at (0,1). Its false-direction probability remains 1/5. With S fixed and whole states sampled independently, the frequency estimator has standard deviation sqrt[p(1−p)/S]. This is a numerical scale, not an interval or probability guarantee. An effective sample size cannot simply replace S for an iid guarantee. No draws are simulated here.
