## Standing objects {#sec-p2-elementary-loss-status}

Fix $y$, the supplied proper law, and an admissible $G$ as in the action-space definitions. This packet is a canonical definition sheet, not a new optimality theorem. Let $M=\binom n2$ and retain $x_{ij}^d(G)=\mathbf1\{g_{id}<g_{jd}\}$ for $i\ne j$.

::: {#def-p2-domain-counts name="Disclosed and erroneous domain comparisons"}
Define
$$
N_d(G)=\sum_{i\ne j}x_{ij}^d(G)
=\sum_{i<j}\mathbf1\{g_{id}\ne g_{jd}\},
\qquad
F_d(G,\Theta)=
\sum_{i\ne j}x_{ij}^d(G)\mathbf1\{\theta_{id}\leq\theta_{jd}\}.
$$
Each unordered pair contributes at most once in one domain. The disclosure count is fixed during posterior integration; the error count is state-dependent. The withheld count is $M-N_d(G)$. Cross-domain disclosures are separate statements.
:::

::: {#def-p2-error-withholding-loss name="Core additive state loss"}
For fixed $w_d\geq0$, $\sum_dw_d=1$, and $0<\tau_d\leq1/2$, define
$$
L_{\mathrm{sep}}(G,\Theta)
=\sum_{d=1}^D w_d L_d(G,\Theta),
\qquad
L_d(G,\Theta)=
\frac{F_d(G,\Theta)+\tau_d\{M-N_d(G)\}}{M}.
$$
The weights are loss weights, not coefficients of a scalar composite target. These losses are bounded measurable functions on the target state space.
:::

::: {#def-p2-additive-posterior-risk name="Core posterior risk"}
The fixed-action posterior risk is
$$
R_{\mathrm{sep}}(G)
=\int L_{\mathrm{sep}}(G,\Theta)\,\Pi_s(d\Theta\mid y).
$$
No moment of the target coordinates is required because $0\leq L_{\mathrm{sep}}\leq1$. For the all-withheld matrix, $F_d=N_d=0$ and $R_{\mathrm{sep}}=\sum_dw_d\tau_d$.
:::

::: {#def-p2-disclosure-utilities name="Separate disclosure summaries"}
Define $I(G)=\sum_dN_d(G)$, the number of disclosed pair-domain statements. A separately declared weighted utility can be
$$
I_v(G)=\sum_{d=1}^D\sum_{i<j}v_{ij}^d
\mathbf1\{g_{id}\ne g_{jd}\},
\qquad v_{ij}^d\geq0,
$$
with fixed finite weights. $I_v$, $I$, $K_d$ and $C(G)$ are different reporting summaries. Maximizing one of them is not the definition of minimizing $R_{\mathrm{sep}}$.
:::
