## Bounded reading and inheritance {#sec-mc-foundation-reading}

Hoeffding's original Theorem 1 supplies a one-sided concentration bound for independent bounded variables. The current reading visually checked printed page 15, equation (2.3) and its endpoint discussion in the existing primary-scan image. Bibliographic identity is the 1963 JASA article, rather than its later online release. [Publisher record](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830).

Clopper and Pearson construct confidence boundaries from binomial tails and discuss their conservative repeated-sampling interpretation. Their zero-count endpoint is determined by a binomial zero-success probability. The relevant scan text was checked at printed pages 404–407 and 410; publisher metadata confirms the 1934 Biometrika identity. [Primary scan](https://www.barestatistics.nl/uploads/1/1/7/9/11797954/clopper__pearson_1934.pdf), [publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article-abstract/26/4/404/291538).

N01 develops the finite-family union argument for P2 report-event indicators. N02 develops a one-sided held-out validation procedure.
